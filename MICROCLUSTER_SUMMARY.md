# Efficient Player Micro-Cluster Analysis Summary

## Executive Summary
Analysis of the **56 total participants** revealed:
- **41 efficient players** (73%) - low retry rate, high first-try accuracy
- **15 error-prone players** (27%) - "the lazy group" with high retries, low first-try accuracy

The 41 efficient players break down into **3 distinct sub-strategies** rather than being a monolithic group.

---

## The 3 Efficient Micro-Clusters

### Strategy 3: High-Earner Deliberators (17 players) 🏆
**Earnings Profile:** $625 avg (highest)

**Key Characteristics:**
- **Advice Dependency:** Heavy visible advice followers (0.88 ratio)
- **Social Style:** Agreement-oriented (64.71%)
- **Decision Making:** Slower & more deliberate (~12 seconds per response)
- **Park Strength:** Plaza Park (99% accuracy)

**Notable Players:**
- Yanita Klomjit ($695)
- Chun Rapat ($692)
- Opal Nutchanan ($684)
- Woraphan Pruchyanimit ($682)

**Interpretation:** This is the **"Trust the system"** strategy. They follow visible advice consistently, prefer agreement-based social environments, and take time to make careful decisions. Their higher earnings suggest that deliberation and following expert guidance pays off.

---

### Strategy 1: Independent Progressives (12 players) 🚀
**Earnings Profile:** $596 avg (mid-tier)

**Key Characteristics:**
- **Advice Dependency:** Lower visible advice follow (0.77 ratio)
- **Learning:** Highest learning delta (0.1098) - they visibly improve over rounds
- **Social Style:** Conflict-oriented/independent (33.33% prefer "against")
- **Decision Making:** Medium speed (~10 seconds)
- **Park Strength:** Plaza Park (96% accuracy)

**Notable Players:**
- Arthi Prasertsuk ($659)
- Sirawit Sriviriyanont ($655)
- Kat Katherine ($643)
- Vanaphol Watanachai ($638)

**Interpretation:** This is the **"Learn & improve"** strategy. They don't blindly follow advice, they actively learn from their attempts, and show strong measurable improvement. They prefer challenging environments (conflict/against) and value figuring things out themselves. Mid-range earnings but with upward trajectory.

---

### Strategy 2: Advice-Purists, Efficiency-Focused (12 players) ⚡
**Earnings Profile:** $537 avg (lowest among efficient)

**Key Characteristics:**
- **Advice Dependency:** PERFECT visible advice follow (1.00 ratio) - follow ALL advice
- **Social Style:** Agreement-oriented (66.67%)
- **Decision Making:** Fastest response time (~9.5 seconds)
- **Advice Frequency:** Lower preference (1.2/3.0) - they avoid high-frequency advice scenarios
- **Park Strength:** Forest Park (94% accuracy)

**Notable Players:**
- Cheng Fang Kleo ($590)
- Suwaphit Dechritthirong ($581)
- Nutt Thanachoksumrit ($570)
- Mark Ekkelenkamp ($567)

**Interpretation:** This is the **"Efficient executor"** strategy. They are maximally efficient (fastest response times, perfect advice following), but they deliberately engage in fewer advice scenarios. Lowest earnings among efficient players, suggesting that speed/efficiency alone doesn't maximize earnings - thoughtfulness matters.

---

## Key Insights

### 1. **High Earnings ≠ Efficiency ≠ Speed**
Strategy 3 (highest earners) are SLOWER than Strategy 2 (lowest earners). The top earners take ~12 seconds vs 9.5 seconds for the efficient executors. **Deliberation beats speed.**

### 2. **Advice Following Has a Sweet Spot**
- Strategy 3 (high earners): 0.88 follow rate ✓ Optimal
- Strategy 2 (lower earners): 1.00 follow rate ✗ Overdoing it
- Strategy 1 (mid earners): 0.77 follow rate ✗ Too low

**The data suggests ~88% advice following is the sweet spot - not rigid, not independent.**

### 3. **Learning Ability Matters**
Strategy 1 shows highest learning delta (0.1098), indicating they improve noticeably through the game. Despite mid-range earnings, having a growth mindset is valuable.

### 4. **Social Preference Clusters**
- High-earners & Purists both prefer "agreement" (social harmony)
- Progressive learners prefer "conflict/against" (they thrive in challenging environments)

### 5. **Park Specialization**
- Deliberators & Progressives: Plaza Park specialists
- Purists: Forest Park specialists
- **Insight:** Different strategies work better in different environments

## Error-Prone Group (The "Lazy" 15 players)

For comparison, the non-efficient group shows:
- High retry rate (0.422 vs 0.088 for efficient)
- Low first-try accuracy (low vs ~0.95+ for efficient)
- High mistakes per round (0.666 vs 0.108)
- Slightly higher final accuracy (0.969 vs 1.000) - suggests they eventually muddle through
- Earnings: $12.71/round vs $14.44 for efficient

**These ARE indeed the "lazy" players** - they make more mistakes initially, retry often, but still manage to complete the game. They don't develop strategies; they brute-force their way through.

## Strategy Recommendations

### If You're a Natural High-Earner (Strategy 3 style):
✓ Take your time - deliberation pays  
✓ Follow visible advice consistently (~88% rate)  
✓ Prefer structured, agreement-based environments  
✓ Focus on Plaza Park specialization

### If You're a Progressive Learner (Strategy 1 style):
✓ Embrace challenge and independent thinking  
✓ Focus on learning, not just winning each round  
✓ Use advice as a guideline, not a rule (77% follow)  
✓ Play in "against" condition scenarios  
✓ Your earnings may be mid-tier but your growth trajectory is strong

### If You're an Efficient Executor (Strategy 2 style):
⚠ Your speed is an asset, but be careful not to over-automate  
✓ Focus 100% on visible advice when you choose to engage  
✓ Deliberately select lower-advice scenarios (1.2/3 frequency)  
✓ Specialize in Forest Park  
⚠ Consider being slightly less rigid to improve earnings
